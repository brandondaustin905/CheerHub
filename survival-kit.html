🥾 Ultimate Competition Survival Kit

Your editable, printable, personalized toolkit for a stress-free competition season. Every tool below saves your changes right in your browser — fill it in once, and it's there next time you visit.

📖 How To Use This Kit

  1. Use the ☰ Menu button in the top-right corner to jump between tools at any time.
  2. Each tool has editable fields — type directly into them just like a form.
  3. Your entries save automatically as you type, right in this browser.
  4. Use the Print button on any tool to print a clean, nav-free copy — great for the Emergency Contact Card and Pre-Competition Routine.
  5. Use the Reset button on a tool if you want to clear it and start fresh (e.g. for a new competition).

How saving works: this kit saves your information using your browser's local storage — meaning it's saved on this device, in this browser, not to an account or the cloud.

  • ✅ Come back to the same browser on the same device, and everything you typed will still be there.
  • ⚠️ Switching devices or browsers means your entries won't carry over automatically.
  • ⚠️ Clearing your browser's history/cache/site data will erase what's saved here.
  • 💡 For anything important long-term (like the Emergency Contact Card), print a copy or take a screenshot as backup.
