📅 Program Design: The 8-Week Prerequisite Chain

The 8-Week Program is criterion-referenced: content unlocks when measured exit criteria pass — never because the calendar says so. The chain below IS the program.

✉️ Email me this link
How to read this page: Plate 3A is the whole program as a chain of locked gates. Plate 3B is what to do when a week won't pass. The table at the bottom shows how to adapt the program without breaking it.

Why gates, not weeks: stunt prerequisites are mechanically real — an extension cannot be pressed off an unstable prep; a liberty cannot balance on an unstable platform. Gates measure actual adaptation; calendars assume average adaptation. The checklist converts "they look ready" into "93% over 40 logged reps."
Why Fall School sits exactly at Week 6, Day 3: fall responses are motor skills — they must be installed under LOW arousal, immediately BEFORE Phase 4 introduces the skills most likely to need them.

Meta-rule: every stall is diagnosed before it is drilled (full method → Figure Brief 4). Repeating a week is the system working. Grinding reps on the wrong cause installs the fault.
