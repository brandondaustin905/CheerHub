🔍 Movement Diagnostics: Finding the Real Cause

Repetition doesn't erode a fault — it consolidates it. Diagnose before you drill. The hierarchy below orders your hypotheses by testing cost and likelihood, so you never blame strength for a timing problem.

Why this order: tightness is checked first because it's the cheapest test and its failure mimics everything else; strength is checked LAST because it's blamed first by instinct, causal least often, and unfixable in-session.

Onset vs endpoint — where the cause lives

FILM STRIP of a posterior-drift fall:

frame 1     frame 2      frame 3        frame 4        frame 5
  |            |         RIB FLARE      lean visible     FALL
 clean       clean       ← ✅ ONSET:     (everyone        ← ❌ endpoint:
                          the cause      corrects here)    the crash
Freeze at onset. The endpoint shows what happened; the onset shows why.
🛡 Error culture note: faults are information, not indictments. Programs that punish reporting (down-calls, "that felt off") destroy the data the diagnostic system runs on. The best diagnostics and the best safety culture are the same culture.
